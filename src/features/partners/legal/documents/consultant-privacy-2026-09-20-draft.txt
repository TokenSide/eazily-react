1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to Easily, a technology-enabled marketplace and service platform operated by United Crew (“United Crew”, “Easily”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

Easily enables independent Consultants and other Service Providers to create profiles, list Services, communicate with Consumers, receive Bookings, manage appointments, and provide Services through the Platform.

This Consultant Service Provider Privacy Policy (“Privacy Policy”) explains how United Crew may collect, use, disclose, store, protect, and otherwise process personal information relating to Consultants and Consultant Service Providers (“Consultant”, “you”, or “your”).

This Privacy Policy applies when you:

Register as a Consultant;
Create or maintain a Consultant profile;
Apply to become a Consultant
Submit verification documents;
List Consultant Services;
Receive or manage Bookings;
Communicate with Consumers;
Receive payments;
Contact Easily support;
Use Consultant tools; or
Otherwise use Consultant functionality on Easily.

By registering for or using Consultant functionality, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
